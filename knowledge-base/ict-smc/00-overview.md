# ICT / Smart Money Concepts — Overview

This folder is my personal reference as I learn ICT / SMC. It is **not** a complete course — just the concepts I am studying, in my own words, with examples from charts I'm watching.

## The core idea (in plain English)

Price is not random. It moves to fill orders. Big institutions need liquidity to enter and exit large positions. They engineer moves that:

1. **Sweep liquidity** — push price into obvious stop-loss zones (above swing highs / below swing lows) to fill their orders against retail traders getting stopped out.
2. **Reverse from key zones** — once enough liquidity is taken, price reverses from areas where institutions left unfilled orders (order blocks, FVGs, etc).
3. **Run to the next pool of liquidity** — the move continues until the next major liquidity pool is reached.

So the SMC trader's job is essentially:
- Identify where liquidity is sitting (highs, lows, equal highs/lows).
- Wait for it to be swept.
- Enter on the reversal from a Point of Interest (POI) — order block, FVG, breaker.
- Target the opposite liquidity pool.

## Concepts to learn (in order)

1. **Market structure** — higher highs/lows, lower highs/lows, BOS, CHoCH.
2. **Liquidity** — buy-side and sell-side, equal highs/lows, trendline liquidity.
3. **Killzones / sessions** — when smart money is active.
4. **Order Blocks (OB)** — last opposing candle before an impulsive move.
5. **Fair Value Gaps (FVG) / Imbalances** — 3-candle inefficiencies price tends to revisit.
6. **Premium / Discount** — only buy in discount, only sell in premium (relative to range).
7. **Power of 3 (PO3)** — accumulation, manipulation, distribution.
8. **Entry models** — Silver Bullet, OTE, Turtle Soup, etc.

## Killzones I trade

These are the windows when most of the day's range is built. Times in **EST**:

| Killzone | Time (EST) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| London Open | 02:00 – 05:00 | High volatility, sets the day's bias for XAU/EUR/GBP |
| NY AM | 07:00 – 10:00 | Best window for NAS100; news at 08:30 often |
| NY Silver Bullet | 10:00 – 11:00 | Tight ICT model — high-probability reversal window |
| NY PM | 13:30 – 16:00 | Secondary opportunity, often continuation |

Outside killzones, price often chops. As a scalper this is when I should **not** trade.
