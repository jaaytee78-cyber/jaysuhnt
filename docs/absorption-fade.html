⚠ The mental model
Price runs into a known level. Aggressive market orders pile in expecting a breakout. Someone bigger sits there with limit orders, eating every print. The aggressors get filled but price doesn't move. They're now trapped. When they realize and cover, the reversal you're trading is their exit liquidity. You are not predicting — you are reading a transaction.

02 · Core Signal — Long Setup
All 5 Conditions Required — LongLong Setup
C1
Location at HTF level
Price tags a predefined level marked at session start: PDH/PDL, weekly H/L, ON H/L, VAH/VAL/POC, daily HVN, VWAP ±1σ/±2σ. The level was on the chart before price got there.
MANUAL
C2
Sell-Side Cluster Absorption
Footprint cluster at bar low shows ask/bid ≥ 4.0 (NQ) / 3.0 (ES). Sellers hitting bids but price not falling — passive buyers absorbing institutional-scale flow.
cluster.AskVolume / cluster.BidVolume ≥ ImbalanceRatio at bar low
LONG
C3
Heavy Delta, No Progress
Bar volume ≥ 1.5× 20-bar avg AND |bar delta| ≥ 70% of bar volume AND body closes in opposite ≤25% of range OR rejection wick ≥ 60%. Aggression failed to move price.
vol ≥ 1.5×avg && |delta| ≥ 70% && (closeHigh ≥ 75% || lowerWick ≥ 60%)
LONG
C4
CVD Divergence (Classic OR Hidden)
Classic: price prints new low vs prior 5–10 bars; CVD does NOT confirm. Hidden: CVD prints new low; price holds above prior low. Either qualifies — hidden divergence catches stealth accumulation classic misses.
classic: low < priorLow && cvd > cvdAtPriorLow ‖ hidden: cvd < cvdAtPriorLow && low > priorLow
LONG
C5
Trigger Bar — Midpoint Reclaim AND Delta Flip
Next 233-tick bar closes back through the midpoint of the absorption bar AND its delta sign is opposite the absorption bar's. Both required. Midpoint reclaim alone can happen on weak retraces; the delta flip filters them.
close > absMid && barDelta > 0 && absorptionDelta < 0
LONG

05 · Honest Assessment — Is This Validated?
What IS Backed by ResearchSolid
ConceptEvidence
Order flow / footprintUsed by prop firms & market makers. Kyle 1985, Glosten-Milgrom models document institutional footprints in bid/ask volume.
CVD divergenceWidely used by professional order flow traders. Delta leading price at turning points is empirically observable in footprint data.
VWAP as referenceGenuinely how buy-side desks and algorithms benchmark execution. Not retail folklore — institutional reality.
Absorption at levelsLarge passive orders absorbing aggressive flow before reversal is a documented, observable microstructure phenomenon.
What Is NOT ValidatedVerify Yourself
⚠The v2 5-condition combination has no published backtest with statistical significance
⚠v2 inherits zero validation from v1 — the 4-phase roadmap applies fresh
⚠Thresholds (4.0/3.0, 1.5×, 70%) are reasonable starting points, not optimised
⚠"75%+ win rates" on trading blogs are marketing figures, not peer-reviewed
⚠Reading absorption has a steep learning curve — paper trade extensively first
BOTTOM LINE
Treat this as a hypothesis to validate. Run the 4-phase roadmap from scratch. Paper → sim → micro → live. Decision gates per phase. Real capital only after Phase 3 passes.

4-Phase Validation · Decision Gates · Persistent State
Validation Roadmap
From hypothesis to live trading. Each phase has a decision gate — if the numbers don't pass, you go back, not forward. Click the status badge on each card to advance: todo → progress → passed → failed → todo. Persists to localStorage.

Goal: Learn to see the v2 setup. No automation. ATAS Replay mode on ES (cleaner) or NQ (your choice). Log 30 instances minimum, walk replay one bar at a time during NY Open / Overlap / NY PM. Every flag — hit, miss, fired-and-failed — gets logged.

⊗ Anti-patterns That Kill Traders Here
Skipping straight to Phase 4 because "code feels productive" — it isn't. You'll backtest an edge you don't understand.
Skipping Phase 3 because backtest looked great — slippage and emotions are not in your backtest.
Sizing up before Phase 4 passes — live edge on micros ≠ scalable edge on full contracts.
Tweaking rules after every losing trade — rules change at decision gates only, with sample size to support the change.
Trading other instruments before AF v2 is profitable on NQ/ES — one edge, one instrument family, until it works.
