⚠ The mental model
Price runs into a known level. Aggressive market orders pile in expecting a breakout. Someone bigger sits there with limit orders, eating every print. The aggressors get filled but price doesn't move. They're now trapped. When they realize and cover, the reversal you're trading is their exit liquidity. You are not predicting — you are reading a transaction.

C4
CVD Divergence (Classic OR Hidden)
Classic: price prints new low vs prior 5–10 bars; CVD does NOT confirm. Hidden: CVD prints new low; price holds above prior low. Either qualifies — hidden divergence catches stealth accumulation classic misses.
classic: low < priorLow && cvd > cvdAtPriorLow ‖ hidden: cvd < cvdAtPriorLow && low > priorLow

Next 233-tick bar closes back through the midpoint of the absorption bar AND its delta sign is opposite the absorption bar's. Both required. Midpoint reclaim alone can happen on weak retraces; the delta flip filters them.
close > absMid && barDelta > 0 && absorptionDelta < 0

How to Use It Alongside the StrategyWorkflow
Score = 5 → Arrow + Alert
Mechanical entry trigger. All 5 confirmed. Enter on close of trigger bar. Set stop 1 tick beyond absorption wick. TP1=1R, TP2=structural. Toggle LevelConfirmed OFF after the trade until next setup.
Score = 4
High probability forming. Hands on keyboard. Pre-plan entry, stop, target. Wait for the 5th — most often that's C5 (trigger bar) or C1 (you forgot to toggle LevelConfirmed).
Score = 3
Conditions building. Pay attention. Check which condition is missing — if C2 cluster absorption is absent, the setup is much weaker (C2 is the footprint signature core). Stay patient.
Score = 0–2
No edge. Ignore entirely. The indicator protects you from false setups as much as it alerts to real ones — a 0 or 1 is valuable information.
⚠ Important Notes & Limitations
Manual C1 toggle: The LevelConfirmed property is the human gate. The indicator can score C2–C5 mechanically but cannot judge whether the price location is meaningful — that's your job. Toggle ON when price reaches a level you marked at session start, OFF after the trade closes.

Cluster data dependency: C2 reads candle.Clusters directly. Some demo feeds don't expose clusters; full Rithmic / CQG feeds do. If C2 always scores 0, your feed isn't supplying footprint data.

CVD anchoring: CVD is currently anchored to chart start, not session start. Acceptable for replay; revisit before live use. Marked // TUNE: in source.

No repainting: Once a bar closes, its score is locked. Signal arrows do not repaint on historical bars. The indicator only reads closed bars (`bar - 1` for prior absorption + `bar` for current trigger).

Paper trade first: Phase 1 manual replay → Phase 2 indicator-assisted replay → Phase 3 live micros → Phase 4 Databento. Confluence scoring is a guide, not a guarantee. v2 inherits zero validation from v1.

Common Mistakes & Corrections
What Goes Wrong & How to Fix ItLearn From These
MistakeCorrection
Entering at score 4/5Wait. The 5th condition is the edge. The whole strategy collapses without full confluence.
Forgetting to toggle LevelConfirmedScore caps at 4. That's by design — you must consciously confirm location. Build it into your pre-entry routine.
Trading 11:30–13:30 ET lunchHard block. Close charts if needed. Low volume + random chop destroys the edge.
Ignoring daily stop limitPre-set a hard stop at the broker level. Mental stops always get moved.
Moving stop against the tradeOnce set, stop does not move until TP1. Wick violated = thesis wrong = take the loss clean.
Skipping the 5-min time stopTrades that haven't worked in 5 min usually aren't going to. Real edge contributor — don't bypass it.
